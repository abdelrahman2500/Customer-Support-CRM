# RM-16 — Email Adapter — Inbound

**Priority:** P2 · **Complexity:** Medium · **Blocked:** Yes · **Phase:** 5 (Email/WhatsApp/SMS)

## Goal

Receive a customer's email reply and correlate it back into the correct
ticket as a `ChannelMessage(EMAIL, INBOUND)`.

## Why it exists

Completes the Email channel's other half — Core 3 names "Email" as a full
channel, not outbound-only.

## Blocked status

Fully blocked on Phase 0's Email decision, and on a second, larger question
Decision Record 1 raises: receiving real mail requires either self-hosting
a real internet-facing mail-receiving server (its own significant ops
undertaking — MX records, spam/abuse handling, TLS) or an inbound-parse
webhook from a commercial provider (typically paid beyond a small free
tier). No zero-cost, low-ops-burden path is confirmed. Do not implement a
fake/simulated inbound path merely to mark this complete.

## Dependencies

`RM-15` (outbound must exist first — the reply-to address a customer emails
back to is generated by the outbound send).

## Backend work (once unblocked)

- A ticket-correlation scheme: either a unique reply-to address per ticket
  (`ticket+<id>@<domain>`) or a subject-line/`Message-ID` token, matching
  how most email-based support systems correlate replies — decide the
  specific scheme once the receiving mechanism (self-hosted vs. provider)
  is chosen, since a provider's inbound-parse webhook may dictate the
  available correlation data.
- `EmailAdapter.parseInbound()` implementation matching the chosen scheme,
  validating the resolved ticket's `branchId` matches the receiving
  mailbox's configured branch before writing the message (tenant-isolation
  requirement from Decision Record 1).

## Frontend work

None beyond what `RM-15` already added — an inbound message renders in the
existing `TicketChatCard` exactly like any other `ChannelMessage`.

## Worker/realtime work

A new inbound-receiving process (self-hosted mail receiver) or a new
webhook route (if a provider is chosen) — shape depends entirely on the
Phase 0 decision.

## Schema/migration work

None beyond `RM-13`'s, unless the chosen correlation scheme needs a new
indexed field (e.g. a `replyToToken` on `Ticket` or `ChannelMessage`) —
decide during implementation once the scheme is chosen.

## Tests

Cannot be meaningfully specified until the receiving mechanism is chosen.

## Acceptance criteria

Cannot be meaningfully specified until the receiving mechanism is chosen.

## Definition of Done

Not applicable until unblocked. This file exists to hold the shape of the
work and its dependency on Phase 0, not to be executed yet.
